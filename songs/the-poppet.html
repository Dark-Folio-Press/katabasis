The Poppet

REENA THE HARPY
A retelling of "Vasilisa the Beautiful" in song — a starving girl, the wooden doll her dying mother left behind, and a night sent into the forest to beg fire from a witch who eats the girls who fail her.
Intro

2, 3, 4

Verse 1

Eat, little doll, eat!

Listen to my grief and meet my need.

I live in my father's house,

yet find no joy.

My cruel stepmother glares

while I get scraps

Response

Weep not, sleep in peace,

the morning is wiser than the evening.

Weep not, sleep in peace,

my little girl.

Pre-Chorus

Eat, little doll, eat!

They sent me to the forest witch tonight,

For a flame, without any light

They think they're rid of my mouth to feed,

Tonight, I die!

Chorus

Eat, little doll, eat!

Take this trembling from me.

Speak, my darling poppet,

Tell me I am not alone.

(you are not alone)

Follow the path ahead,

and bring us home.

Verse 1.5

She was waiting at the gate,

wrapped in a shawl that smelled like smoke and sap.

No broom, no cackling,

just a look that sized me up.

"Work for me till the moon turns," she said,

"sort the needles, split the kindling, scrub the floor.

Do it clean.

If you fail, little girl,

I'll eat you whole before dawn.

The witch turned her heel and walked into the night.

Verse 2

The doll's eyes sparked

like coals under a blown-out pot.

It moved beside me, quick and quiet,

all ember-edge and grit.

It worked the blackened floor,

Split the kindling, sorted the needles

Response

Weep not, and wake now,

the morning is wiser than the evening.

and the witch will be here at daylight

Be brave and follow my lead

Check the door!

Verse 3

the forest witch snored,

heavy as a log in the next room.

I eased the door, I held my breath,

I slipped past the little house on chicken legs.

The yard had skulls on poles,

teeth catching the moon.

[Whispered] I whimpered,

the doll said, "Now, girl.

Snag one for the road.

and don't look back."

Bridge

I took the skull lantern,

a jaw with a guttering grin.

I held it by a dangling spine

And the poppet said, "Bring it."

I carried that dead, white glow

like a warning with teeth inside.

Final Verse

I reached the cottage I had been sent from,

skull held high, its glare lighting the porch.

The latch gave once. The door stood open.

By dawn there was no sound, no step, no woman at the hearth

Just a fist of ash on the boards

and two diamond earrings,
