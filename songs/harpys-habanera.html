Harpy's Habanera

REENA THE HARPY
A translation and rewrite of "L'amour est un oiseau rebelle" — the Habanera from Bizet's Carmen (1875) — pulled out of the 19th century and stripped down to something modern, blunt, and a little dangerous.

Love is a rebellious bird

that nobody can tame,

No point in calling it

If it doesn't care to come nothing helps.

No Hope, No Prayer

One man talks well, the other's quiet;

it's the other one that I prefer.

He's silent but I like the way he looks.

Love! Love! Love! Love!

Love is a feral child,

it has never, ever, known a law;

love me not, then I love you;

if I love you, you'd best beware!

The bird you thought you had

beat its wings and flew away …

When love stays away, you wait and wait;

when least expected, there it is!

All around you, swift, so swift,

it comes, it goes, and then returns …

you think you hold it fast, it flees

you think you're free, it holds you fast.

Love! Love! Love! Love!

Love is a feral child,

it has never, ever, known a law;

love me not, then I love you;

if I love you, you'd best beware!
